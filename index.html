About

Hi! I'm Atira Todd and I am currently a second year Computer Games Programming student at the University of Staffordshire. I have experience with Unity and Unreal Engine and in languages such as C++ and C# as well as with libraries and APIs such as SDL 2.0 and DirectX11. I enjoy participating in game jams and have completed 6 so far, both independently and as part of a team.

Projects

Graphics Engine

This is the project I worked on as part of my second year Real Time Rendering module. It utilises various graphics and rendering techniques and concepts such as per-pixel Phong shading, loading and texturing objects, alpha clipping and cameras. Additionally, it utilises specialised vertex and pixel shaders to create things such as a skybox.

Time Frame: 8 weeks

Team Size: 1

Languages/Libraries Used: C++, HLSL, DirectX 11

Space Fight

This is a game I made by myself during the Multiplayer Games Programming module in my second year of university. It is a multiplayer game that uses a server-client model to allow multiple players to play the game at the same time, transmitting data between the server and the different clients using TCP and UDP protocols. It is a Space Invaders inspired game where the players have to defeat enemy ships during a set time without being defeated themselves.

Time Frame: 8 weeks

Team Size: 1

Language/Library Used: C#, Monogame

Secrets of Stillwater Lake

This is a game I made by myself for the Fishing Horror Jam. The game was created over the course of 11 days with a focus on fishing as a central mechanic of the game. It is set in the fictional place of Stillwater Lake, somewhere that seems normal on the surface but, after fishing around, you'll start to uncover some of the secrets lurking beneath the waves.

Time Frame: 11 days

Team Size: 1

Language/Engine Used: C#, Unity

After Dark - UKIE Student Games Jam Submission

This is the game I made on my own for the 2025 UKIE Student Games Jam where the theme was Contrast. It is a platformer game where the player has the ability to switch between light and dark and must use this ability in order to traverse through the levels.


Time Frame: 36 hours

Team Size: 1

Language/Engine Used: C#, Unity

Tank Game

This is a game I made as part of my first year Mechanics Protyping module. It is a game where you play as a tank and must traverse through your environment, defeating enemies using various kinds of weapons along the way, before finally taking on a boss battle in order to escape.

Time Frame: 8 weeks

Team Size: 1

Language/Engine Used: Blueprint, Unreal
